# 🚀 Quick Start - YouTube Music Player

## What Changed?
✅ Replaced Spotify integration with simple YouTube player  
✅ No OAuth, no API keys, no configuration needed  
✅ Just paste any YouTube link and play!  

## How to Use

### 1. Start Your Study Session
```
1. Login to Study Pulse
2. Set your study preferences
3. Click "Get Study Plan"
4. Click "Confirm & Start Timers"
```

### 2. Play Music
```
1. YouTube Music Player appears below the timer
2. Paste any YouTube link
   Example: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jfKfPfyJRdk
3. Click "Play"
4. Music starts automatically!
```

### 3. Control Music
- Use YouTube's built-in controls (play, pause, volume, etc.)
- Music plays independently from the timer
- Can pause music without affecting timer
- Can pause timer without affecting music

### 4. Save for Later
- Your last played link is automatically saved
- Next time you start a session, just click "Play" again!

## Tips

💡 **Find Good Study Music**: Search YouTube for:
- "lo-fi study music"
- "focus music"
- "study beats"
- "concentration music"
- "ambient study"

💡 **Copy Link from YouTube**:
1. Go to any YouTube video
2. Click "Share"
3. Click "Copy"
4. Paste in Music Player!

💡 **Playlists Work Too**: 
- YouTube playlist links work
- Will play the first video
- Use playlist controls to navigate

💡 **Volume Control**:
- Use YouTube's volume slider
- Or your system volume
- Independent from app sounds
